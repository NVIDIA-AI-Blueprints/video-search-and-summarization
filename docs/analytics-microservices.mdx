The Analytics Microservices process and enrich the metadata streams produced by the
vision microservices, transforming raw detections into actionable insights and verified
alerts.

- [Behavior Analytics ](/behavior-analytics) - Tracks objects over time across camera sensors, computes behavioral metrics, and detects spatial events and rule-based incidents.
- [Alerts Microservice ](/alert-verification-service) - Ingests alerts and incidents, retrieves the corresponding video segments, and uses Vision Language Models to verify alert authenticity.
