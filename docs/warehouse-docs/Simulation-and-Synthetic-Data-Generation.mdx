Simulation and synthetic data generation (SDG) are increasingly central techniques to augment the dataset of perception models and
to provide a digital twin for end-to-end development & validation of applications.

The goal of SDG is to generate a flexible number of well-placed cameras that provide full scene coverage,
along with corresponding ground truth data, to support Multi-Camera Multi-Target Tracking (MTMC) use cases.
Detailed dataset introduction can be found in our released [PhysicalAI-SmartSpaces](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nvidia/PhysicalAI-SmartSpaces).
